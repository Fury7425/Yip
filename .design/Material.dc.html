How it maps to WinUI 3

  • Do not give each card its own blur. Build one CompositionEffectFactory (backdrop → Gaussian blur → saturate → tint) in BuildCompositionLayer() and hand the same CompositionEffectBrush to every card’s backing SpriteVisual. Shader compilation is the expensive part and it happens once.
  • The pill needs no effect at all — it is its own window, so DesktopAcrylicBackdrop gives it the blur at window level, for free.
  • Specular edge is a 1 px CompositionNineGridBrush over a vertical CompositionLinearGradientBrush, not a stack of box-shadows.
  • Elevation is a composition DropShadow on the card visual, not a painted border.

Per meter tick, at 60 Hz

1FFI call — rec_meter(), already lock-free
3clip-rect writes (peak, RMS, hold)
2waveform writes — newest bar + container offset
0Measure / Arrange passes
0brush, animation or string allocations
0blur re-evaluations — see below

THE ONE THAT BITES A blur re-runs whenever its source changes. Put the waveform and the meter in a sibling visual above the blurred card, never inside the surface the effect samples — otherwise every level update repaints a 28 px blur over the whole card at 60 Hz. Cards never move, so the compositor caches the blurred result until the window does.
